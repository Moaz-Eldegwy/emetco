Equipment schedule: eight product families, one supplier.

Each family carries a tag you can add straight to your RFQ. Add the families you need, then tell us the size, rating and quantity.

VA01

Valves & Actuators

Gate, globe, check, butterfly, ball, plug, knife gate and control valves. Pneumatic, electric and hydraulic actuators. Positioners, limit switches and solenoid valves.

PM02

Pumps & Motors

Submersible, centrifugal, dosing and metering, chemical injection, diaphragm, screw and piston, boiler feed pumps. AC, DC and special motors. Star/delta, soft starters and VSDs.

IN03

Instrumentation & Analytical

Flowmeters, pressure meters and temperature transmitters. Sensors, barriers, signal converters and interface relays. Measuring and calibration devices.

AC04

Automation & Control

PLC, DCS and SCADA control cards, drives and turbine spare parts from ABB, Siemens, GE, Honeywell and Emerson.

CB05

Cables

Control, instrument and fire alarm cables. Marine, high-temperature and chemical-resistant cables.

EX06

Explosion-Proof & Safety

Explosion-proof lighting and ATEX control panels (Eaton CEAG). Fire alarm and fire fighting systems.

BI07

Burner Ignition & Flame Systems

US-made and built to spec: igniters, flame scanners, oil and gas guns. OEM replacement parts for FPS, Chentronics, Coen, Peabody, B&W, Forney, Alstom and GE.

TF08

Tank Fittings, Relief Valves & Cathodic Protection

Pressure/vacuum relief valves, flame arresters, gauge hatches and radar level gauges for storage tanks. Cathodic protection systems.
