FAQ

Quick answers.

What products do you supply?

Valves and actuators, pumps and motors, instrumentation, automation and control (PLC, DCS, SCADA), cables, explosion-proof and safety equipment, burner ignition systems, tank fittings, relief valves and cathodic protection. We also supply pipes, fittings and expansion joints.

Which brands can you supply?

Brands we supply include ABB, Siemens, Emerson (Fisher, Rosemount), Endress+Hauser, Schneider Electric, Honeywell, GE, Eaton CEAG, Flowserve, KSB, Grundfos, Rotork and many more.

How do I request a quote?

Fill the quote form, send a WhatsApp message to +20 10 9965 4547, or email info@emetcoeg.com. A part number, brand and model, or a photo of the nameplate is enough to start.

Can you find spare and replacement parts?

Yes. We supply OEM replacement parts for burner and flame systems, and PLC, DCS and SCADA control cards and turbine spare parts from brands such as ABB, Siemens, GE, Honeywell and Emerson. Send the part number and we will check it for you.

Which industries do you work with?

Oil and gas, petrochemicals, power generation, water and wastewater, utilities, manufacturing, marine and offshore, and the food industry.

Where are you located?

Our office is at 30 Street 270, New Maadi, Cairo, Egypt.
