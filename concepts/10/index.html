03 / About Emetco

Engineers first. Suppliers second.

Founded by engineers with 25 years in electrical, mechanical and control equipment.

Emetco is a 100% Egyptian company, founded by a group of engineers with 25 years of experience in electrical, mechanical and control equipment.

We are an engineering services, trading and contracting company in electrical and mechanical control systems, instrumentation and process. We supply high-quality products, services and solutions that meet international standards, for industry, oil & gas, petrochemicals, power, utilities and manufacturing.

We deliver cost-effective solutions through a wide network of trusted international partners, backed by an experienced local team.
