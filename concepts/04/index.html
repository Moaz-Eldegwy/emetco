Our story

100% Egyptian. Founded by engineers.

Emetco was founded by a group of engineers with 25 years of experience in electrical, mechanical and control equipment. We believe a plant deserves a supplier who understands the equipment, not just the price list.

Today we supply high-quality products that meet international standards to oil & gas, petrochemicals, power, utilities and manufacturing, through a wide network of trusted international partners and an experienced local team.
