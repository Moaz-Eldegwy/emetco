Use the quote form at the top of this page, email your list to info@emetcoeg.com, or send it on WhatsApp to +20 10 9965 4547. Part numbers, brands or a datasheet all help.

We supply 40+ international brands, including ABB, Siemens, Emerson (Fisher, Rosemount), Endress+Hauser, Schneider Electric, Honeywell, GE, Eaton (CEAG), Flowserve, KSB, Grundfos, Rotork, Spirax Sarco and more.
